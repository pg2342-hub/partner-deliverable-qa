Partner deliverable QA

Within co-delivers through consulting partners: the partner's team runs a 30-day discovery sprint on a customer's month-end close, and Within's delivery lead makes sure their work meets Within's standard before it reaches the customer's controller. This page is that check. Partners can also run it on their own work before handing it over.

How to read it

  1. Pick a version below: the partner's first draft, or the same work after the partner fixed it.
  2. The verdict says whether it is ready for the controller.
  3. If not, "What goes back to the partner" lists each problem in plain words.
