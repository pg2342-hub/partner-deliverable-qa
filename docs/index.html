Partner deliverable QA

Who uses it: Within's delivery lead, on a consulting partner's work, before the controller readout.

Within co-delivers through consulting partners. The partner's consultants run the discovery sprint for a customer, and Within's delivery lead holds what they produce to Within's standard before it reaches the customer's controller. This is that check. It reads the partner's process map, ranked fixes and sign-off record for a 30-day month-end close sprint, checks them against the close calendar, and returns a verdict, a send-back list for the partner, and the fixes ranked by hours saved per unit of effort. Partners can also run it on their own work before handing it over.

The scenario follows my 30-day close sprint plan: a corporate close plus an acquired business unit's close. All data is synthetic: 38 calendar tasks, a partner draft with 9 planted problems, and the same deliverable after the partner fixes them. Code on GitHub.
